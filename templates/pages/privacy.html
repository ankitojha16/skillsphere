Beta Transparency Notice

SkillSphere Privacy Policy & Data Practices

Effective Date: November 2026 • Private Beta Release
1. Overview & Beta Context

SkillSphere is currently in an invite-only private beta release for initial student cohorts. We treat student privacy with the highest engineering rigor, adhering to principles of strict data minimization, purpose limitation, and storage security. This policy outlines exactly what information is collected, how it is used, and how you maintain total control over your learning data.

2. What We Collect and Why
  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your full name, email address, and an encrypted password hash. We never store raw passwords.
  • Learning Preferences: During onboarding, you may provide your technical level, education background, interests, and career goals to personalize course suggestions.
  • Course Progress & Work: We track lesson completion, video watch milestones, flashcard intervals, quiz answers, and coding challenge submissions so you can continue your education seamlessly across sessions and earn accredited completion certificates.
  • First-Party Product Telemetry: To identify broken workflows and improve educational content, we record first-party application events (such as course views, enrollments, and lesson completion). These logs use internal database IDs and never contain passwords, auth tokens, personal emails, or sensitive query strings.
3. Zero Third-Party Data Selling

SkillSphere never sells, rents, or monetizes student data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or external trackers. All telemetry is 100% first-party and hosted within our private database cluster.

4. Telemetry Opt-Out

You have the right to opt out of non-essential product usage analytics at any time. You can toggle this setting in your Profile Settings or during initial profile setup. When opted out, our telemetry system skips event recording for your account.

6. Age Safeguards & Minor Protections

SkillSphere is engineered for university, secondary school, and adult career learners. The platform is not intended for children under 13 years of age. Students between 13 and 17 must participate with parental or guardian consent where mandated by regional laws.

7. Legal Compliance Disclaimer

This privacy disclosure represents the current operational behavior of the SkillSphere application during private beta. While engineered with international privacy best practices, this platform does not claim automated formal certification under GDPR, CCPA, or FERPA without final independent legal counsel, which will be completed prior to global public commercial launch.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about your privacy or wish to request manual record removal, please submit a report via the Beta Feedback portal or contact the engineering team directly.
